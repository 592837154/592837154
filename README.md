## 📖 我的 IT 历程 / The Journey
*“像写日记一样，记录技术生涯的每一个足迹。”*

### 🕒 2018 - 2021: 扎根与成长 (Deep Roots)
- **CN**: 18年入行前端。那时的我是一名“菜鸟”，为了跟上节奏，我将全部精力都投入到了工作中。那时候，GitHub 对我来说是遥远的，我的代码都留在了公司的业务里。
- **EN**: Started my frontend journey in 2018. As a beginner back then, I poured all my energy into daily work to sharpen my skills, leaving little time for open-source contributions.

### 🌑 2022 - 2024: 消失的坐标 (The Lost Years)
- **CN**: 22年的一次误操作，导致我的邮箱拉黑了 GitHub 官方邮件，我彻底失去了账号的控制权。那两年的 GitHub 是一片空白，仿佛我的技术生命失去了一个重要的坐标。
- **EN**: A mistake in 2022 led to my email provider blacklisting GitHub, causing me to lose access to my account. Those years were a "dark age" for my GitHub profile—a void in my digital map.

### ✨ 2025: 重逢与复苏 (The Reunion)
- **CN**: 感谢人工智能 Gemini。25年通过 AI 的图像识别与分析能力，我终于找回了账号。此时的我已能轻松应对工作，开始偶尔写点代码作为娱乐。
- **EN**: Thanks to Gemini AI, I recovered my account in 2025 through image analysis. With work challenges now well within my grasp, I began coding for fun on GitHub once again.

### 🚀 2026 - Present: 认真记录，不留遗憾 (Commit to History)
- **CN**: 今天我意识到，我需要认真记录。不仅是为了职业目标，更是为了回首往事时，能看到自己思考过、努力过的痕迹。这里将成为我 IT 生涯的“双语数字资产库”。
- **EN**: Today, I’ve realized the importance of intentional recording. Not just for career goals, but to leave a trail of my thoughts and efforts. This is now my digital diary and a bilingual asset library of my IT life.
